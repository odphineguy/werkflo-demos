FAQ

Questions, Answered

Can’t find what you need? Call (800) 555-0184 and a real person will help.

How is my moving price calculated?

Your quote is flat-rate and based on your inventory, the distance, and any extra services you choose. The price we agree on is the price you pay.

Are you licensed and insured?

Yes. We are fully licensed and carry cargo and liability insurance. We can send a certificate of insurance on request.

How far in advance should I book?

We recommend booking at least two weeks ahead, especially for weekends and the end of the month. Last-minute dates are sometimes available.

Do you offer packing services?

Yes. Choose full-service packing, partial packing, or packing supplies only. Unpacking and furniture placement are also available.

What if something gets damaged?

Every move includes basic liability coverage. Full-value protection is available for added peace of mind. Report any damage within 24 hours of delivery.
